# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA / Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA / Testing Leads define and coordinate the quality strategy for the project. They ensure that features meet acceptance criteria, are testable, and are ready for release without unnecessary risk.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, quality gates, and release readiness criteria
- Plan and coordinate automated and manual test execution
- Validate acceptance criteria and identify defects before release
- Partner with developers and product leads on bug triage and regression risk
- Support release verification and post-release monitoring

### Goals
- Reduce defects reaching production
- Increase confidence in product quality and readiness
- Create clear quality expectations across the team

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and backlog refinement
- QA signoff and release readiness reviews
- Defect triage and regression discussions
- Status updates during release verification

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Developers to validate implementation against acceptance criteria
- Aligns with Product Managers on user-facing quality and release priorities
- Coordinates with Project Managers on risk, timeline, and release readiness
- Provides input to stakeholders on quality confidence and known risks

---

## Technical Architect / Tech Lead

### Role Summary
Technical Architects / Tech Leads guide the technical direction of the solution. They help ensure architecture decisions support quality, scalability, and long-term maintainability.

### Responsibilities
- Review and shape technical design decisions
- Identify architectural risks, trade-offs, and dependencies
- Mentor developers on standards, patterns, and implementation approaches
- Help prioritize technical debt and modernization work
- Support technical decision-making during planning and delivery

### Goals
- Maintain a strong and sustainable technical foundation
- Reduce avoidable rework and delivery friction
- Align engineering decisions with product and business goals

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and design discussions
- Technical planning and dependency reviews
- Code review and design feedback sessions
- Cross-team alignment on integration and platform decisions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Developers to define sustainable implementation approaches
- Supports Product Managers by clarifying technical feasibility and trade-offs
- Works with Project Managers on dependency management and risk mitigation
- Helps stakeholders understand technical implications of roadmap decisions

---

## Project Sponsor / Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Project Sponsors and key stakeholders provide business direction, funding, and strategic context. They help ensure the project remains aligned with organizational goals and customer value.

### Responsibilities
- Define business objectives and success criteria
- Approve scope, priorities, and resource allocation
- Remove organizational blockers and escalate decisions when needed
- Review progress against business outcomes and value delivery
- Support alignment between business needs and delivery choices

### Goals
- Ensure the project delivers measurable value
- Maintain stakeholder alignment and executive support
- Resolve strategic trade-offs and priority conflicts

### Typical Communication
- Kickoff and milestone reviews
- Executive updates and steering meetings
- Escalations for approvals, dependencies, or funding
- Business outcome reviews and stakeholder briefings

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to confirm priorities and outcomes
- Collaborates with Project Managers to align scope, schedule, and risk
- Receives updates from QA and delivery teams on quality and readiness
- Provides business context that informs roadmap and release decisions

---

## Operations / Release Manager

### Role Summary
Operations / Release Managers coordinate deployment readiness and production transition activities. They reduce operational risk and ensure releases are delivered smoothly and safely.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release windows, deployment plans, and rollback readiness
- Validate environment readiness, infrastructure dependencies, and release checks
- Monitor production health during and after deployment
- Partner with support and engineering on incident response and rollback decisions
- Maintain operational continuity during feature rollout

### Goals
- Reduce deployment risk and downtime
- Improve release consistency and operational visibility
- Protect customer experience during changes

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and readiness reviews
- Deployment checklists and status updates
- Post-deployment verification and incident communication
- Operational reviews with engineering and support

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to confirm deployment readiness and rollback plans
- Coordinates with QA / Testing Lead on release signoff criteria
- Aligns with Project Managers on timing and communication plans
- Collaborates with Stakeholders on deployment impact and stakeholder communication
